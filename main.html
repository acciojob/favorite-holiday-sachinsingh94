Maldives


The Maldives is a tropical paradise in the Indian Ocean, famous for its crystal-clear waters, white sandy beaches, and luxurious resorts. It is the perfect destination for relaxation, adventure, and romance.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Male City – the vibrant capital with cultural landmarks.
  2. Banana Reef – one of the best diving spots in the Maldives.
  3. Sun Island – known for its stunning beaches and water sports.
  4. HP Reef – a protected marine area with colorful corals.
  5. Maafushi Island – a popular local island with authentic experiences.

Must-Try Foods

  • Mas Huni – shredded tuna with coconut, onion, and chili.
  • Garudiya – a traditional fish soup served with rice.
  • Fihunu Mas – grilled fish marinated with Maldivian spices.
  • Saagu Bondibaiy – a sweet sago pudding with coconut milk.
  • Bis Keemiya – a Maldivian snack similar to spring rolls.
